# Quickest path to a registered tax agent lodging Quillo returns

> Researched 2026-10-24 against the TASR 2022 compilation in force 1 Oct 2026 (F2026C01004) and
> tpb.gov.au pages, fetched directly. **Assumes** the founder has no accounting qualification or
> tax-practice experience. General information only, not legal or tax advice.

## Bottom line

The founder can't be the approver soon. Every pathway needs **at least 1 year full-time (or
part-time equivalent) under a registered tax agent**, plus TPB-approved tax law and commercial law
courses ([TASR Sch 2 Pt 2](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2022L00238/latest)). An unrelated
degree helps only as the entry ticket to a **postgraduate award in accounting** (item 201, which
needs 12 months of experience instead of 24). With a degree that is about 18–24 months; without
one, 2.5–4 years. The fastest credible route is **(b)**: Quillo Pty Ltd registers as a company
agent with **one contracted registered agent** as its "sufficient number". The TPB's minimum is
"at least one", and contractors count
([TPB(GS) 53/2024](https://www.tpb.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-04/TPB%28GS%29%2053_2024_Supervision%2C%20competency%20and%20quality%20management%20under%20the%20Tax%20Agent%20Services%20Act%202009.pdf) ¶19, ¶22).
That gets a first lodgement in about 2–4 months, and the founder's supervised work there counts
toward his own registration.

## Routes

| Route | Time to first lodgement | Cost (AUD) | Effort | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Founder qualifies | 18–24 mo with a degree; 2.5–4 yr without | Courses $2.7k–$13k+; fee $290; PI | Very high | Away from Quillo; experience rejected |
| (b) Quillo company + contracted agent | ~2–4 mo (TPB aims for 30 days, up to 60) | Fee $290; retainer; PI; SBR software | Medium | Quillo becomes a tax practice; registration depends on the agent staying |
| (c) Partner firm | Weeks | Agent fee, paid by the user | Low–medium | Partner dependency; referral disclosure |

## Individual pathways (Sch 2 unchanged since 2022)

| Item | Qualification | TPB courses | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 201 | Degree **or postgrad award in accounting** | Tax + commercial law | 1 yr in 5 |
| 202 | Non-accounting degree **relevant to the services** | As many as the Board decides | 1 yr in 5 |
| 203 | Diploma+ in accounting | Tax + commercial law | **2 yr** in 5 |
| 204 | Law | Accounting + tax law | 1 yr in 5 |
| 205 / 206 | None / association member | All three / none | 8 yr in 10 |

Items 207–211 cover financial advisers and legacy cases and don't apply.

- **What counts as experience:** work as an agent, under an agent's "supervision and control", as
  a lawyer, or work "of another kind" the Board approves. Simulated programs count for at most
  15% ([TPB](https://www.tpb.gov.au/relevant-experience-tax-agents)).
- **The 1,400-hour figure is the BAS-agent test** under item 101
  ([TPB](https://www.tpb.gov.au/qualifications-and-experience-bas-agents)).
- **An unrelated degree such as CS doesn't qualify under 202.** It must be relevant to the
  services, and the 202 guidance targets conditional registrations
  ([GS 19/2012](https://www.tpb.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-06/TPB%28GS%29%2019_2012_Tertiary%20qualifications%20in%20a%20discipline%20other%20than%20accounting%20for%20tax%20agents.pdf) ¶2–3).
- **Course costs:**
  - The 5 TPB units: $2,700. FNS50222 Diploma of Accounting with tax electives: $3,234, over
    18–24 months ([Applied Education](https://www.appliededucation.edu.au/bookkeeping-accounting/diploma-of-accounting/)).
    "FNS50322" is the mortgage-broking diploma, not the accounting one.
  - The Tax Institute's Grad Dip of Applied Tax Law: 6 × $2,215, and it is TPB-qualifying with the
    CommLaw electives ([TTI](https://www.taxinstitute.com.au/education/programs/graduate-diploma-of-applied-tax-law)).
- **Changes 2024–26:**
  - Treasury's post-PwC review consulted on the pathways
    ([Treasury](https://treasury.gov.au/consultation/c2024-536402)), but Sch 2 has not been
    amended. The amendments since 2022 cover fees, the register and sanctions.
  - The 2024 Code Determination has applied since 1 Jul 2025 to practices with 100 or fewer staff
    ([TPB](https://tpb.gov.au/code-professional-conduct)).

## Obligations on the approver

- **Registration:** fit and proper, PI cover ($250k / $500k / $1m by turnover) and 120 h of CPE
  every 3 years ([fit & proper](https://www.tpb.gov.au/fit-and-proper-requirements),
  [PI](https://www.tpb.gov.au/pii),
  [CPE](https://www.tpb.gov.au/continuing-professional-education)).
- **Code s30-10 and the Determination:** best interests, confidentiality, reasonable care,
  engagement letters, quality management and supervision.
- **Client identity:** PN 5/2022 is archived and replaced by
  [GS 42/2022](https://www.tpb.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-07/TPB%28GS%29%2042_2022_Proof%20of%20identity%20requirements%20for%20client%20verification.pdf),
  updated for AML/CTF in July 2026. The client also signs a declaration before each lodgement.
- **PLS:** needs registration, myID, RAM and **SBR-enabled software**
  ([ATO](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-and-super-professionals/digital-services/practitioner-lodgment-service/practitioner-lodgment-service-user-guide/getting-access-and-setting-permissions)).
  If the agent lodges from existing practice software, Quillo needs **no DSP status**. That is an
  inference, and it holds only while Quillo never calls the ATO itself.
- **Conflict of interest:** owning both the app and the practice is a conflict to disclose and
  manage. The subscription motive sits against the agent's best-interests duty, and client data
  moving between the app and the practice needs consent.
- **BAS agent registration** is quicker but useless here: SBR IITR marks BAS agents "N".
