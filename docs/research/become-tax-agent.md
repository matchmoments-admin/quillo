# Quickest path to a registered tax agent lodging Quillo returns

> Researched 2026-10-24 against the TASR 2022 compilation in force 1 Oct 2026 (F2026C01004) and
> tpb.gov.au pages fetched directly. Builds on `lodgement-options.md` (branch
> `research/lodgement-options`) and `docs/first-timer/lodgement-routes.md`. **Assumes** the founder
> holds no accounting qualification or tax-practice experience. General information only, not
> legal or tax advice.

## Bottom line

The founder can't be the approver soon. Every individual pathway needs at least **1 year
full-time (or part-time equivalent) under a registered tax agent**, plus TPB-approved tax law and
commercial law courses ([TASR Sch 2 Pt 2](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2022L00238/latest)).
A degree in an unrelated field only shortens this if it lets him enrol in a **postgraduate award
in accounting** (item 201): 12 months of experience instead of 24. That makes about 18–24 months
realistic, and 2.5–4 years with no degree. The fastest credible route is **(b)**: Quillo Pty Ltd
registers as a company tax agent with **one contracted registered agent** as its "sufficient
number". The TPB's minimum is "at least one", and the person can be a contractor
([TPB(GS) 53/2024](https://www.tpb.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-04/TPB%28GS%29%2053_2024_Supervision%2C%20competency%20and%20quality%20management%20under%20the%20Tax%20Agent%20Services%20Act%202009.pdf) ¶19, ¶22).
First lodgement in about 2–4 months. The founder's supervised work there also counts toward his
own registration.

## The three routes

| Route | Time to first lodgement | Cost (AUD) | Effort | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) Founder qualifies | 18–24 mo with a degree; 2.5–4 yr without | Courses $2.7k–$13k+; fee $290; PI | Very high: study plus 1–2 yr FTE in a practice | He's away from Quillo; TPB may reject the experience |
| (b) Quillo company + contracted agent | ~2–4 mo (find agent; TPB "aim 30 days, up to 60"; ATO access) | Fee $290; agent's retainer; PI from $500k cover; SBR practice software | Medium: supervision system, Code compliance | Quillo becomes a tax practice; registration fails if the agent leaves (ongoing requirement) |
| (c) Partner agent firm | Weeks | The agent's fee, paid by the user | Low–medium | Partner dependency; referral disclosure |

## Individual tax-agent pathways (TASR Sch 2 Pt 2, unchanged since 2022)

| Item | Qualification | TPB courses | Experience |
|---|---|---|---|
| 201 | Degree **or postgraduate award in accounting** | Tax law + commercial law | 1 yr FTE in last 5 |
| 202 | Degree/postgrad award, non-accounting, **relevant to the services** | As many as the TPB decides | 1 yr in 5 |
| 203 | Diploma or higher in accounting (RTO) | Tax law + commercial law | **2 yr** in 5 |
| 204 | Law (legal practitioner academic quals) | Basic accounting + tax law | 1 yr in 5 |
| 205 | None | All three | 8 yr in 10 |
| 206 | Voting member of a recognised association | None | 8 yr in 10 |

Items 207–211 are financial-adviser and legacy pathways and don't apply here.

- **Experience** means work as an agent, under an agent's "supervision and control", as a lawyer,
  or work "of another kind" the Board approves. Simulated programs count for at most 15%.
  "General accounting only" is unlikely to count
  ([relevant experience](https://www.tpb.gov.au/relevant-experience-tax-agents)).
- **The hour figures (1,400 h and so on) are the BAS-agent tests:** 1,400 h in 4 years under item
  101 ([BAS](https://www.tpb.gov.au/qualifications-and-experience-bas-agents)). The tax-agent test
  is counted in years.
- **A CS or other unrelated degree doesn't qualify under 202.** It has to be relevant to the
  services, and the TPB's 202 guidance targets *conditional* registrations
  ([GS 19/2012](https://www.tpb.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-06/TPB%28GS%29%2019_2012_Tertiary%20qualifications%20in%20a%20discipline%20other%20than%20accounting%20for%20tax%20agents.pdf) ¶2–3, ¶9).
- **Courses:** the 5 TPB units (FNSSS00005 + FNSSS00008) cost $2,700; FNS50222 Diploma of
  Accounting with tax electives costs $3,234, over 18–24 months
  ([Applied Education](https://www.appliededucation.edu.au/bookkeeping-accounting/diploma-of-accounting/)).
  The brief's "FNS50322" is a finance and mortgage-broking diploma; FNS50222 is the accounting
  one. The Tax Institute Grad Dip of Applied Tax Law is $2,215 × 6 subjects and is "TPB
  qualifying" with the CommLaw electives
  ([TTI](https://www.taxinstitute.com.au/education/programs/graduate-diploma-of-applied-tax-law)).

**Changes 2024–26:**

- Treasury's post-PwC registration review consulted on removing association pathways and widening
  experience ([Treasury](https://treasury.gov.au/consultation/c2024-536402)), but **Sch 2 hasn't
  been amended**. The 2024–26 amendments covered fees, the register and sanctions.
- The 2024 Code Determination has applied since 1 Jul 2025 to practices with ≤100 staff
  ([TPB](https://tpb.gov.au/code-professional-conduct)).

## Obligations on the approver

- **Registration:** fit and proper, PI insurance (tiers $250k / $500k / $1m by turnover), and
  120 h of CPE per 3 years
  ([fit & proper](https://www.tpb.gov.au/fit-and-proper-requirements),
  [PI](https://www.tpb.gov.au/pii),
  [CPE](https://www.tpb.gov.au/continuing-professional-education)).
- **Code s30-10 and the Determination:** honesty, best interests, confidentiality, reasonable
  care, letters of engagement, quality management, supervision.
- **Client identity checks:** PN 5/2022 is archived and replaced by
  [TPB(GS) 42/2022](https://www.tpb.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-07/TPB%28GS%29%2042_2022_Proof%20of%20identity%20requirements%20for%20client%20verification.pdf),
  aligned with AML/CTF in July 2026. The agent also needs the client's signed declaration before
  each lodgement.
- **PLS:** needs a TPB registration, a Digital ID (myID), RAM, and **SBR-enabled software**
  ([ATO PLS access](https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-and-super-professionals/digital-services/practitioner-lodgment-service/practitioner-lodgment-service-user-guide/getting-access-and-setting-permissions)).
  If the agent lodges from existing practice software, Quillo is a data source and **needs no DSP
  status**. That is an inference: it only applies while Quillo doesn't talk to the ATO itself.
- **Conflict of interest:** owning both the app and the practice is a conflict that has to be
  disclosed and managed. Quillo is selling a subscription while the agent owes the client a
  best-interests duty. Sharing client data between the company and the app needs consent.
- **A BAS agent registration is faster** (Cert IV, GST/BAS course, 1,400 h) **but useless**. SBR
  IITR accepts BAS agents as "N".
