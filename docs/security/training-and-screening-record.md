## 2. Background screening

**National police check** through an **ACIC-accredited body**. Find one from the ACIC National Police
Checking Service page: https://www.acic.gov.au/services/national-police-checking-service (follow the
link to the list of accredited bodies; many offer an online check within 1–3 business days for about A$40–50).
Alternatively apply through your state or territory police.

Purpose to select: employment / "other — commercial engagement" (or the category the provider offers for
company directors or financial services). Fiskil's "fit and proper persons" check may also ask about
bankruptcy (search the AFSA National Personal Insolvency Index) and director disqualification (ASIC).
